# Citation and positioning review

All 19 cited works were checked against primary sources. The review concerns the scope of attribution and bibliographic metadata.

- **power2022grokking**: Introduces delayed generalization on small algorithmic datasets. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02177)
- **nanda2023progress**: Uses functional Fourier ablations and restricted/excluded losses; framing revised to credit these methods. ICLR 2023 header verified. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.05217)
- **thilak2022slingshot**: Adaptive-optimizer training cycles and grokking. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.04817)
- **varma2023explaining**: Ungrokking continues training on a reduced dataset; circuit-efficiency account. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.02390v1)
- **prieto2025grokking**: Softmax collapse and logit-scaling loss-minimization direction. ICLR 2025 venue verified. [Primary source](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/c9e6ac15e689e06139d7b39e1667b165-Abstract-Conference.html)
- **prakash2025grokking**: HiLD 2025 paper describes late test collapse with perfect training accuracy. [Primary source](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=upyC7rwR1M)
- **zhong2023clock**: Different learned algorithms under different configurations; NeurIPS 2023 acceptance recorded. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17844)
- **jordan2024muon**: Original Muon optimizer and recommended citation authors/year. [Primary source](https://github.com/KellerJordan/Muon)
- **liu2025muon**: Large-scale Muon uses AdamW for embedding/head parameters; weight-spectrum analysis is distinguished from operand Fourier dispersion. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16982v1)
- **tveit2025muon**: Reports grokking acceleration in the tested configurations. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16041)
- **wang2026active**: Studies orthogonalization, iteration count, and accuracy dips; prior instability is explicitly acknowledged. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20512)
- **chughtai2023toy**: Shared algorithm families with circuit variation; wording revised away from universal identical circuits. [Primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/chughtai23a.html)
- **elhage2021framework**: Residual-stream basis freedom motivates the parameterization symmetry; it does not establish the optimizer trajectory. [Primary source](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html)
- **ruan2026muon**: Robustness and higher effective feature rank; observable distinguished from this paper Fourier count. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09658)
- **wang2025tailend**: Associative-memory performance and isotropic learned weight spectra. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26030)
- **anthes2023diagnosing**: Readout misalignment in continual learning; proceedings pages 748-751 verified. [Primary source](https://2023.ccneuro.org/proceedings/0000748.pdf)
- **chou2026twospeeds**: Relative representation/readout learning speeds during grokking. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27078)
- **gomezjurado2026longdelay**: Decoder bottleneck in one-step Collatz prediction; distinguished from the two-speeds study. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13082)
- **liu2026glitching**: Finite-precision class-sum error, feature inflation, and zero-sum projection; credit retained and author metadata normalized. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06152)
