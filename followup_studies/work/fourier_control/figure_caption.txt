Fourier filtering can supply task structure to a lookup-only predictor. (a) A one-hot lookup memorizes the 3,830 training answers and returns zero on all 8,939 held-out inputs. Full addition-family projection with DC reaches 100% accuracy on every split. Family ablation retains DC and leaves training predictions correct, giving 0% held-out and 29.99% full-grid accuracy. Bars show means across five original splits; whiskers span the seed range. (b) A lookup normalized by training class counts reaches 100% accuracy after retaining any one nonzero diagonal conjugate pair and DC. The ordinary lookup's diagonal pair powers are mathematically tied, so the plotted prefixes use increasing frequency as a fixed tie break. Lines show seed means and shading spans the seed range. Both panels use p=113 and the repository's original 30% training splits.
