# Related work and the contribution supported by this study

Prepared September 22, 2026, while the new arithmetic continuations are running. Their outcomes are intentionally left open. The prior-paper summaries below each use fewer than 200 words. This is a comparison with two directly relevant primary sources; it is not an exhaustive novelty search.

## Directly relevant prior results

**Prieto, Barsbey, Mediano, and Birdal (2025).** They identify absorption errors in softmax cross-entropy that round the correct-class derivative to zero while wrong-class updates can continue. Their experiments connect this condition to arrested generalization and show that precision changes and StableMax can alleviate it. They also analyze a prediction-preserving logit-scaling direction and introduce an optimizer intervention against that direction. Their main modular-arithmetic setup usually omits weight decay; additional comparisons examine regularization. These results already establish the relevance of correct-class derivative cancellation and loss arithmetic to grokking. Accurate evaluation of the existing CE objective in our study differs mathematically from replacing its exponential probability transform with StableMax. [Grokking at the Edge of Numerical Stability, version 2, sections 2 to 5 and appendix H](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04697v2).

**Liu Hanqing, Cao, Li, and Zhou (2026).** They connect correct-class derivative cancellation to a broken zero-sum constraint, coupled classifier/feature-mean drift, gradient re-emergence, and amplified Adam steps producing loss spikes. Their principal experiments use Adam with zero weight decay. They report mitigation from higher-precision loss computation and explicitly test subtracting each example's mean logit gradient. Their theoretical feedback analysis uses conditions including approximate neural-collapse geometry, class-balanced aggregation, and slowly varying residual probability mass; its classifier update derivation uses gradient descent. Those conditions require separate examination for our split-Muon/AdamW model with decay. Their work substantially overlaps a proposed numerical explanation of our failures and must receive direct credit. [Grokking or Glitching? How Low-Precision Drives Slingshot Loss Spikes, version 2, May 12, 2026, sections 3 to 4 and appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06152v2).

## What our completed evidence contributes

The useful contribution is a measured account of what fails and what survives in a specified optimizer configuration. A claim that this study discovers cancellation in CE, establishes the first numerical explanation of such spikes, or introduces zero-sum projection would misstate its relationship to the work above.

1. **Retained generalizing information during severe failure.** Training-only probes recover 98.20 to 100% held-out accuracy from the selected five-seed collapsed representations. Report these values alongside their healthy-reference probes and the native failure accuracies. This answers an information-preservation question that the native model's accuracy cannot answer by itself.
2. **Adjacent causal localization.** Four combinations of preceding/current features and readouts show that the updated readout reproduces most acute damage, while updated features remain useful with the preceding readout. Exact parameter hybrids and saved-state update analysis locate the destructive function change within the actual captured update.
3. **A test of the residual-basis account.** Full-domain existence diagnostics, train-only transfer, matched healthy intervals, and planted network gauges distinguish exact architecture symmetry from the observed trajectory. They reject a literal global basis explanation for the measured pairs while quantifying useful approximate compensation.
4. **The split-Muon/AdamW setting with explicit decay.** The implemented hidden, embedding, and readout groups use distinct update rules and nonzero decay. This is a concrete setting for testing the arithmetic mechanism. Attribute the resulting effect to the tested configuration; a Muon-specific claim would require a matched optimizer contrast that isolates Muon.
5. **Spectral and functional distinction.** The paper's Fourier analyses can be presented alongside the new decoder and head-swap results to distinguish information in a representation from its accessibility through the current readout. Exact support, power distribution, and native filtered accuracy have different mathematical meanings.

These are contributions of the present empirical study. We have not established that every individual analysis is unprecedented across the broader literature.

## How the new branches will change the wording

If the accurate-loss and target-derivative-repair branches suppress the registered failures, describe a controlled numerical contribution to the observed post-grokking instability in this regularized split-optimizer system. If row-mean projection also suppresses them, report that specific replicated intervention with credit above. If target repair succeeds while projection fails, report the distinction between removing the common component and correcting the target derivative. If corrected arithmetic still fails, characterize that event using the same accurate-gradient and adjacent-head diagnostics, then scope the arithmetic conclusion to the measured cases.

The additive contribution can survive a revision of the original explanation: severe native-model failure can coexist with highly decodable task representations, and component interventions reveal how the interface loses its function. Those claims already have direct evidence. The new arithmetic trials decide their upstream causal context.
